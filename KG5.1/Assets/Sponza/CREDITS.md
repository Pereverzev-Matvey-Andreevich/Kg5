- [Source archive for the textures and material assignments](https://github.com/jimmiebergmann/Sponza/tree/222338979d32f4f4818466291bdbc29f192b86ba)

KG5.1 keeps the existing OBJ geometry. The texture files were converted
from TGA to PNG, preserving the embedded opacity masks. Images above
1024 pixels on either side were resized. The MTL keeps the original
material names and points to the PNG albedo and normal maps.
